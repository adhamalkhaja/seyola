## [2.0.0] — 2026-04-20

**Major:** Ship 10 new skills across 3 new departments. Seyola goes from 7 skills (Advisor + Research) to 17 skills (Advisor + Research + Marketing + Sales + Operations). Matches the 1 Advisor + 4 Departments promise.

**New — Marketing department:**
- `/brand-voice` — Extract or build a consistent voice profile. Foundation for all content.
- `/content-strategist` — Hummer Protocol content engine blueprint. Pillars, sophistication matrix, Hamoor Protocol, offer rhythm.
- `/linkedin-writer` — LinkedIn posts using the Perspective-Shifting Code and Hell-Heaven-Bridge framework.
- `/humanizer` — 24-pattern final pass. Called automatically as terminal step by linkedin-writer, email-sequences, offer-architect.

**New — Sales department:**
- `/offer-profiler` — Five Ps, PFC avatar, "I Help" statement. Niche profile as foundation.
- `/offer-architect` — 15-section Offer Document. 5 components, roadmap, pricing, proof, signature IP.
- `/offer-check` — Scores any offer against 16 iterations that generated $1M+ (Aug 2024 – Feb 2026).

**New — Operations department:**
- `/email-strategist` — Email Engine Blueprint. ESP selection, DNS, magnet stack, Trigger-Tag-Sequence architecture.
- `/email-sequences` — Welcome, conversion, launch, nurture, re-engagement, post-purchase sequences.
- `/email-xray` — Scores broadcasts against 153-broadcast dataset (250+ clients, $1M+) and 8,182 emails from 47 creators.

**New — knowledge/ folder:**
- 7 foundational reference files (brand, content, copywriting, email, offer, strategy, growth) shared across departments.

**Deprecated from the public download page:**
- Engineering department (qa, ship, review, investigate) — not built. Removed from site copy to match what actually ships.
