# Email Skills

The engine that turns subscribers into customers. Strategy, sequences, individual emails, and ongoing health monitoring — the complete email lifecycle.

## Skills

- [[email-strategist]] — plan the complete email engine: ESP selection, DNS setup, magnet stack, automations, capture system. Produces an HTML Email Engine Blueprint.
- [[email-sequences]] — build complete sequences: welcome (7-email arc), conversion (4-7), launch (6-10), nurture, re-engagement, post-purchase. Produces an HTML Sequence Plan.
- [[email-writer]] — write individual emails using the 111 Framework (1 Big Idea + 1 Story + 1 Action), 8 subject line formulas, cadence strategy. Produces ready-to-send email copy.
- [[email-health]] — audit email performance: 8 core KPIs, traffic-light scoring, re-send strategy, retargeting pipeline, quarterly protocol. Produces an HTML Health Report.

## When to use email skills

- Setting up email infrastructure from scratch
- Building or upgrading a welcome sequence
- Writing weekly broadcasts
- Running a launch email campaign
- Diagnosing deliverability problems
- Quarterly email health check

## The Email Flow

1. [[email-strategist]] builds the engine — ESP, DNS, magnets, automations, capture
2. [[email-sequences]] creates the sequences — welcome, conversion, launch, nurture
3. [[email-writer]] writes individual emails — broadcasts, promos, 9-word hand-raisers
4. [[email-health]] monitors and maintains — KPIs, re-send, purge, quarterly protocol

Strategy → Sequences → Writing → Health. Build it, fill it, run it, maintain it.
