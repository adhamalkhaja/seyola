# Brand Skills

The complete brand-building engine. These skills turn your methodology, stories, and expertise into a content machine that attracts premium buyers — from strategic planning through to daily LinkedIn posts and paid amplification.

## Skills

- [[content-strategist]] — plans the complete content engine. Hummer Protocol flywheel, content pillar architecture (50/50 growth/conversion), Market Sophistication Matrix (3 lenses), Hamoor Protocol for premium positioning, Monopoly Brand framework (Big Idea + Charisma + New Model), 5 Content Laws, Cult Code, offer rhythm. HTML Content Engine Blueprint.
- [[story-architect]] — builds your signature stories and Authority Diamond. Four core conversion stories (Genesis, Hero's Journey, Culture, Customer) with the 7-beat Signature Story Formula, Five Horsemen of elite marketing, personal content strategy. HTML Signature Story Bank.
- [[linkedin-writer]] — writes LinkedIn posts in your exact voice/style. Perspective-Shifting Code (What/Why/Where/What Next), Hell-Heaven-Bridge framework, Post Master Structure (5-step creation process), psychographic injection, Whisperer Effect. Ready-to-post content or HTML LinkedIn Batch.
- [[content-amplifier]] — scales content through authority jacking, growth content replication, retargeting ads, and ManyChat automation. Scale Tax (15-20% reinvestment), organic-to-ad pipeline, 30-day amplification calendar. HTML Content Growth Playbook.

## Skill Flow

```
content-strategist → story-architect → linkedin-writer → content-amplifier
     (plan)            (raw material)     (produce)         (scale)
```

1. **[[content-strategist]]** defines what to create — pillars, targeting, rhythm
2. **[[story-architect]]** builds the stories that power all brand content
3. **[[linkedin-writer]]** produces posts using strategy + stories + voice
4. **[[content-amplifier]]** amplifies what works through paid + automation

## When to use brand skills

- Planning your content strategy and pillar architecture
- Building signature stories for your brand
- Writing LinkedIn posts in a specific voice/style
- Scaling organic content with retargeting and ads
- Defining your Monopoly Brand (Big Idea + New Model)
- Targeting premium buyers (Hamoor Protocol)
- Setting up content automation (ManyChat, stories)

## Dependencies

- [[brand-voice]] should be defined first — voice profile feeds into [[linkedin-writer]] and [[story-architect]]

- [[lead-magnet]] powers capture in [[content-amplifier]] — ManyChat triggers need magnets
