# Content Skills

Create once, distribute everywhere. These skills cover organic content — SEO articles, newsletters, and cross-platform distribution.

## Skills

- [[content-atomizer]] — takes one piece of source content and creates platform-optimized versions for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Covers format specs, hook formulas, and algorithm signals for each platform.
- [[seo-content]] — full articles optimized for search that read like a human wrote them. Takes a keyword or cluster and produces publication-ready content with proper E-E-A-T signals.
- [[newsletter]] — multiple formats: roundup, deep-dive, personal essay, curated links, news briefing. References patterns from Lenny Rachitsky, Morning Brew, Greg Isenberg, Sahil Bloom, The Hustle.

## When to use content skills

- Building organic traffic through SEO
- Maintaining a newsletter
- Repurposing content across social platforms
- Creating a content calendar
- Maximizing distribution of existing content

## Dependencies

- [[keyword-research]] feeds into [[seo-content]] — research keywords before writing
- [[brand-voice]] should be defined first — voice consistency across all content
- [[content-atomizer]] works on any source content — blogs, newsletters, videos, podcasts

## Connected Clusters

- **[[brand]]** — [[linkedin-writer]] produces posts that [[content-atomizer]] distributes to other platforms; [[content-amplifier]] scales organic content through paid amplification
