# Growth Skills

The engine that builds your audience. Keywords and lead magnets that drive traffic and opt-ins.

## Skills

- [[keyword-research]] — strategic keyword research using the 6 Circles Method. Expands from seed keywords, clusters into content pillars, maps to a prioritized content plan. No expensive tools required.
- [[lead-magnet]] — generates 3-5 lead magnet concepts with hooks, formats, and clear bridges to the paid offer. Covers info products, SaaS, and services patterns.

## When to use growth skills

- Planning content strategy around keywords
- Creating a lead magnet for a new offer
- Building traffic to your opt-in pages

## The Growth Flow

1. [[keyword-research]] reveals what your audience searches for → content gaps become opportunities
2. [[lead-magnet]] turns those gaps into opt-in offers → builds the list
3. [[content-atomizer]] distributes across platforms → drives more traffic to the magnet

## Connected Clusters

- [[email]] — once they opt in, the email engine takes over (strategy → sequences → writing → health)
- [[positioning-angles]] helps position the lead magnet with the right hook
- [[direct-response-copy]] principles apply to lead magnet copy
