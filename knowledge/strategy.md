# Strategy Skills

Before you write a single word, you need to know your angle. Strategy skills help you find the positioning that makes everything else work.

## Skills

- [[orchestrator]] — the hub that routes you to the right skill based on your situation. Start here when unsure.
- [[positioning-angles]] — surfaces 3-5 positioning options using frameworks from Dunford, Schwartz, Hormozi, and direct response tradition. Every angle includes the psychology of why it works and when to use it.

## When to use strategy skills

- Launching a new product or offer
- Marketing isn't converting and you don't know why
- Entering a new market or niche
- Need to differentiate from competitors
- Starting any marketing project from scratch

## Flow

Strategy skills feed into everything else. Once you have your angle:
- [[direct-response-copy]] turns it into sales copy
- [[lead-magnet]] builds a top-of-funnel around it
- [[brand-voice]] ensures the angle matches your voice
