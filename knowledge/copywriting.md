# Copywriting Skills

The words that make people buy. These skills cover everything from headlines to full sales pages, grounded in 100 years of direct response tradition.

## Skills

- [[direct-response-copy]] — the complete copywriting system. Headlines, opening lines, curiosity gaps, flow techniques, CTAs, testimonials, founder stories. References Schwartz, Hopkins, Ogilvy, Halbert, Caples, Sugarman, and Collier.
- [[brand-voice]] — two modes: Extract (analyze existing content) or Build (construct a voice from scratch). Outputs a voice profile that feeds into all content skills.

## When to use copywriting skills

- Writing a landing page or sales page
- Crafting email subject lines and body copy
- Creating ad copy for paid campaigns
- Punching up existing copy that isn't converting
- Defining how your brand should sound

## Dependencies

- Best used after [[positioning-angles]] — the angle determines what you say, copy determines how you say it
- [[brand-voice]] should be defined before heavy copy production — consistency compounds

## Connected Clusters

- **[[brand]]** — [[content-strategist]] defines what to write about; [[story-architect]] provides conversion stories for copy; [[linkedin-writer]] uses [[positioning-angles]] for post angles and [[brand-voice]] for consistency
