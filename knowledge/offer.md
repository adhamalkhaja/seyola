# Offer Skills

Skills for building, researching, and launching your offer. The four skills follow the arc: **Profile → Research → Build → Launch**.

## Skills

### [[offer-profiler]] — Niche Profiler
Define who you serve with crystal clarity. Five Ps (One Person, One Pain, One Promise, One Path, One Platform), 4-layer Perfect Future Client avatar (demographics, psychographics, behavioral, emotional triggers), "I Help" statement, and journey stage assessment. Produces an HTML Niche Profile.

**Use when:** you need to define your niche, identify your ideal client, pick platforms, or write your positioning statement.

### [[offer-strategist]] — Market Strategist
Research your market and build your signature system. **Web-powered** — uses WebSearch and WebFetch to find real competitors, analyze their positioning, and surface actual pain points from Reddit, YouTube, forums. 12 Pain Drivers assessment, root motivation classification, 3×3×3 Painkiller Program, and signature system naming. Produces an HTML Market Strategy.

**Use when:** you need market research, competitor analysis, pain driver assessment, or want to name your signature system.

### [[offer-architect]] — Offer Architect
Build the complete offer and the 15-section offer document — the single most important sales asset in the business. 5 offer components (Product, Promise, Urgency, Bonuses, Guarantee), client roadmap with phases, 5-page playbook structure, pricing strategy (PIF, Split, MRR), proof collection (Four Questions), and signature IP (Three Pillars). **Works standalone** — gathers minimum info if no prior skills done. Produces an HTML Offer Document.

**Use when:** you need to build an offer, write an offer document, set pricing, collect proof, or build your IP.

### [[offer-launcher]] — Launch Commander
Design the complete launch playbook. Five Cs experience scorecard, onboarding system with timeline, delivery stack (community, resource hub, calendar), 5-stage launch sequence (Tee-Up → Pre-Launch → Launch → Last Call → Post-Launch), 5-post content plan with 1-1-1 framework, urgency strategy, and IP deployment across marketing, sales, and delivery. Produces an HTML Launch Playbook.

**Use when:** you need to plan a launch, set up client experience, design onboarding, or deploy your IP.

## Flow

```
offer-profiler → offer-strategist → offer-architect → offer-launcher
   (who)           (market)           (build)           (launch)
```

1. **Profile** — define who you serve (PFC avatar, Five Ps)
2. **Research** — understand the market (competitors, pain drivers, signature system)
3. **Build** — construct the offer (15-section document, pricing, proof, IP)
4. **Launch** — design the go-to-market (experience, onboarding, launch sequence)

Each skill works standalone, but they're strongest in sequence. The offer-architect gathers minimum context if you skip the first two.
