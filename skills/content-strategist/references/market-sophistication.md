# Market Sophistication Matrix

Your audience isn't one blob. They sit at different levels of awareness, competence, and buying readiness. Content that works for one level fails for another. This matrix uses 3 lenses to map your audience so you create the right content for the right people.

## Lens 1: Awareness Levels (Eugene Schwartz)

The classic 5-stage awareness pyramid. Each level needs different content:

| Level | State | What They Need | Content Type |
|-------|-------|---------------|-------------|
| 1. Unaware | Don't know they have a problem | Show the problem exists | GROWTH — "Why X is silently killing your business" |
| 2. Problem Aware | Know the problem, not the solution | Name the pain | GROWTH — "The real reason your content isn't converting" |
| 3. Solution Aware | Know solutions exist, comparing | Differentiate your approach | CONVERSION — "Why most content strategies fail (and what works instead)" |
| 4. Product Aware | Know about your offer specifically | Prove it works | CONVERSION — Case studies, testimonials, authority jacking |
| 5. Most Aware | Ready to buy, need a push | Make the offer | CONVERSION — Direct offer, urgency, scarcity |

**The key insight**: Growth content targets levels 1-2 (brings new eyeballs). Conversion content targets levels 3-5 (shifts beliefs and closes). The [[hummer-protocol]] 50/50 split ensures you're feeding both ends.

## Lens 2: Competence Levels (Dunning-Kruger Effect)

Where your audience sits on the competence curve determines what content they respond to:

**Mount Stupid** — They just learned about the topic and think they know everything. Overconfident beginners. They consume "hack" and "secret" content. Dangerous to sell to — they don't know what they don't know.

**Valley of Despair** — They tried, failed, and now doubt everything. Overwhelmed. They've consumed too much content and can't execute. They need clarity, not more information. They need [[perspective-shifting-code]] content (WHERE to look, not HOW to do it).

**Slope of Enlightenment** — They're implementing, seeing early results, and hungry for optimization. They need frameworks, not basics. Best clients. They want insights, not value.

**Plateau of Sustainability** — They've figured it out and want to scale. They need specialized, advanced strategies. Highest-paying clients. Hamoors live here.

**Where to focus**: Most profitable content targets the Valley and Slope. These people are ready to invest because they've felt the pain of doing it wrong. Mount Stupid people aren't ready. Plateau people already have solutions.

## Lens 3: Buying Behavior (The Hamoor Protocol)

Three buyer segments with completely different psychology:

### Planktons (avoid attracting)
- Beginners, freebie-seekers, low or no budget
- Consume all your free content, never buy
- Ask "how much?" before "what is it?"
- Attracted by: value-based content (templates, swipe files, A-Z playbooks, walkthroughs, hacks)
- **Revenue share**: Many of them, almost no revenue

### Fish (acceptable, not ideal)
- Mid-level, implementing, needs proof before buying
- Will buy if they see enough social proof and case studies
- Ask "does this work?" and "show me results"
- Attracted by: case studies, testimonials, comparison content
- **Revenue share**: Moderate numbers, moderate revenue

### Hamoors (your target)
- Wealthy, values time over cost
- Doesn't need convincing — needs to trust YOU specifically
- Asks "when can we start?" and "what's the fastest path?"
- Attracted by: insight-based content (stories, proof, findings, lessons, outcomes)
- **Revenue share**: Few of them, most of the revenue

**The critical distinction**: Value-based content attracts Planktons. Insight-based content attracts Hamoors. See [[hamoor-protocol]] for the 11 types of Hamoor Bait.

## Mapping Your Content

For each piece of content, ask:
1. **Which awareness level am I targeting?** (determines growth vs conversion)
2. **Which competence stage am I speaking to?** (determines depth)
3. **Which buyer am I attracting?** (determines value vs insight approach)

### Growth Content (Levels 1-2)
- Hook: Problem-naming, myth-busting, contrarian takes
- Format: Short-form video, carousels, threads
- Goal: Maximum reach and new followers
- Metric: Views, shares, new follows

### Conversion Content (Levels 3-5)
- Hook: Belief-shifting, proof, authority
- Format: Long-form, authority jacking, case studies, stories
- Goal: Trust and DM conversations
- Metric: Saves, DMs, strategy calls booked

## Content Targeting Cheat Sheet

| If your audience is mostly... | Create more... | With this tone... |
|------------------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Unaware + Mount Stupid | Growth (viral, entertaining) | Provocative, attention-grabbing |
| Problem Aware + Valley | Growth → Conversion bridge | Empathetic, clarity-focused |
| Solution Aware + Slope | Conversion (proof, frameworks) | Expert, systematic |
| Product Aware + Plateau | Conversion (direct offers) | Confident, exclusive |
