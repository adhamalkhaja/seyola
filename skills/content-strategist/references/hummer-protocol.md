# The Hummer Protocol (One Simple Flywheel)

The Hummer Protocol is a systematized content-to-client engine. Not random posting. Not "be consistent and hope." A flywheel with clear stages, content types, and conversion mechanics.

## The Flywheel

```
GROWTH CONTENT (50%) ──→ brings eyeballs (strangers → followers)
        ↓
CONVERSION CONTENT (50%) ──→ shifts beliefs (followers → trust)
        ↓
PERSONAL CONTENT (throughout) ──→ builds relatability (trust → connection)
        ↓
LEAD MAGNETS ──→ captures contact (connection → subscriber)
        ↓
OFFER RHYTHM ──→ closes the deal (subscriber → client)
        ↓
        └──→ repeat (client success → more growth content)
```

Each stage feeds the next. Skip one and the flywheel breaks.

## Content Pillars: The 50/50 Split

Out of every 10 pieces of content:

**5 Growth Content pieces** — target [[market-sophistication]] levels 1-2 (unaware + problem aware):
- Steal viral hooks from competitors (80% hook, 20% your spin)
- Algorithm-friendly formats (short-form video, carousels, threads)
- High shareability — the content people send to friends
- Topics: "why" content, problem-naming, myth-busting, hot takes

**5 Conversion Content pieces** — target [[market-sophistication]] levels 3-5 (solution → most aware):
- Authority jacking (reaction videos to expert podcasts)
- Case studies and client transformations
- Belief-shifting stories using [[signature-stories]]
- Testimonials with context (before state + after state)
- "How I did X" breakdowns (proof, not theory)

**Personal Content (blended throughout)** — not a separate pillar, but woven into everything:
- The mundane daily life (reality TV approach)
- Cast members, locations, props
- Vices and guilty pleasures (relatability)
- Behind-the-scenes of business and life
- See [[personal-content]] for the full framework

## Scale20 Standards (The Non-Negotiables)

These are the baseline metrics before optimizing anything:

| Standard | Minimum | Target |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Content creation time | 100 min/day | — |
| Short-form content | 5/week (M-F) | 7/week |
| Long-form content | 1/week | 2/week |
| Email list growth | 4 leads/day | 20 leads/day |
| DM conversations | 20/day | 50/day |
| Strategy calls | 5/week | — |
| Offers made | 1/day | — |

**The Rule of 100**: Master a skill through 100 reps before optimizing. Don't judge content performance before 100 pieces. The first 100 are learning. The next 100 are refining. The 100 after that start compounding.

## Offer Rhythm (4-Week Cycle)

Content without an offer rhythm is charity. The [[hamoor-protocol]] demands a structured monthly cycle:

- **Week 1**: Core Offer Launch (your main high-ticket)
- **Week 2**: Customer Offer Pre-Launch Promo (mid-ticket tease)
- **Week 3**: Customer Offer Launch (mid-ticket open)
- **Week 4**: Client Offer Pre-Launch Promo (high-ticket tease for next month)

Every week has its purpose. Content maps to where you are in the cycle.

## The Content-to-Revenue Pipeline

```
ORGANIC (5/week short-form + 1/week long-form)
  ↓
LEAD MAGNETS (email mini-course, community, quiz, comment triggers)
  ↓
EMAIL ENGINE (welcome → nurture → conversion sequences)
  ↓
DM CONVERSATIONS (genuine, not sales-scripted)
  ↓
STRATEGY CALLS (5/week minimum)
  ↓
OFFER (one per day — in content, email, or DM)
```

## Implementation Sequence

1. **Week 1-2**: Define [[monopoly-brand]] (Big Idea, Cult Code, New Model)
2. **Week 3-4**: Map audience with [[market-sophistication]] (3 lenses)
3. **Week 5-8**: Start posting (growth + conversion mix, don't judge yet)
4. **Week 9-12**: Analyze what works, double down on winners
5. **Month 4+**: Add [[content-amplifier]] tactics (retargeting, authority jacking)
