# The Hamoor Protocol

Hamoors are the wealthy buyers who value time over cost. They don't need convincing — they need to trust you. The Hamoor Protocol is 7 behaviors that position you for premium, 10 mistakes that repel them, and 11 types of content (Hamoor Bait) that specifically attract high-ticket buyers.

## The 7 Hamoor Behaviors

### Behavior 1: Every Offer You Make Should Be Exclusive
Don't sell to everyone. Create urgency and scarcity that's real — limited spots, limited time, invitation-only. Hamoors pay premium for exclusivity. If anyone can buy, no one wants to.

### Behavior 2: Define An Offer Rhythm & Follow It
Monthly 4-week cycle (see [[hummer-protocol]]):
- Week 1: Core Offer Launch
- Week 2: Customer Offer Pre-Launch Promo
- Week 3: Customer Offer Launch
- Week 4: Client Offer Pre-Launch Promo

Consistency signals stability. Hamoors want to buy from people with systems, not people who launch whenever they're desperate.

### Behavior 3: Make It Clear You Took Things Away
When doors close, they close visibly. Update your profile. Post about it. Make people feel what they missed. The FOMO is real and it drives future action.

### Behavior 4: Put The Ring In The Bio When You Close Doors
Your profile should announce exclusivity. When your offer closes, your bio reflects it: "Currently closed — waitlist open." This signals demand. Hamoors want what others can't easily get.

### Behavior 5: Follow Up Genuinely & Never Chase
After a strategy call, follow up once after 2 business days if waiting on a decision. Be genuine. Never chase. The energy of chasing repels Hamoors. Send the offer details, then step back. If they're a Hamoor, they'll come back.

### Behavior 6: Keep Your Waitlist Always Ready
Maintain more demand than supply. When spots open, notify waitlist 24 hours before going public. This VIP treatment signals premium. Hamoors love being on the inside.

### Behavior 7: Don't Create Content You Don't Enjoy Creating
Energy matters. If you hate making Reels, don't make Reels. If you love writing, write. Hamoors can sense forced content. Create from genuine enthusiasm — the right people will find you.

## The 10 Mistakes That Repel Hamoors

1. **Talking To Leads & Not Clients** — Creates neediness. Your content should speak to people who've already decided to buy, not people you're trying to convince.

2. **Treating Everyone Like Your Friend** — Friend-zoning your audience. Be friendly, not their friend. Professional warmth, not buddy energy.

3. **Promising The Same Thing Twice** — Weakens your offer. If you keep recycling the same promise, it signals nothing has changed.

4. **Calling Out Your Niche** — "Hey coaches!" screams desperation. Use the Whisperer Effect instead: lay out the problem, let the right people self-identify. See [[perspective-shifting-code]].

5. **Labelling Yourself Into A Box** — "I'm a business coach" limits you. Use identity instead: share your vision, your mission, your cause. Let people label you.

6. **Getting Defensive & Aggressive** — When someone criticizes, Hamoors watch how you respond. Aggression repels premium buyers. Rise above.

7. **Shaming Or Trying To Make People Guilty** — "You should be further along" messaging shows weakness, not authority. Inspire, don't shame.

8. **Choosing A Niche Like Everyone** — Let your niche choose you. Share big ideas, your [[monopoly-brand]] code, your philosophy. People self-segment based on resonance.

9. **Always Asking Your Audience What They Want** — Leaders lead. Don't poll your audience on what to create. Create from conviction. Hamoors follow vision, not consensus.

10. **Calling Out Negatively** — "Those gurus who..." energy repels. You can be contrarian without being negative. Throw rocks at enemies (ideas, not people).

## The 11 Hamoor Baits

Content types that specifically attract premium buyers:

### 1. Big Plans & Updates
Monthly updates on your big plan. Share the vision, progress, next milestones. Hamoors invest in people with a trajectory, not people standing still.

### 2. The Cult Code
Your rules and values. "In my world, we..." and "We don't do X." Creates an us-vs-them identity that premium buyers want to belong to. See [[monopoly-brand]].

### 3. Merging Ideas With Products & Services
Lead with ideas, not products. "I believe X, and that's why I built Y." The idea sells the product. Never the reverse.

### 4. Demonstrating Proof
Show the lifestyle. Show the results. Not bragging — demonstrating. Hamoors want to see that the destination is real before they buy the map.

### 5. Insights For Buyers, Value For Triers
**Critical distinction**: Value-based content (templates, swipe files, how-to) attracts Planktons. Insight-based content (stories, findings, lessons, perspectives) attracts Hamoors. Give insights, not instruction manuals.

### 6. Show How It Works
Before/after comparisons. "Here's what it looked like before... here's what it looks like after." Hamoors respond to transformation evidence.

### 7. Show The Problem Source
Don't just name problems — explain WHY they happen. Root cause analysis positioned as content. "The reason your content isn't converting isn't your consistency — it's your pillar split."

### 8. Contextual Case Studies & Testimonials
Not just "Client X got Y result." Include the before context: where they were, what they'd tried, why it wasn't working, and then the transformation. See [[signature-stories]].

### 9. Sharing & Expanding Highlights
Past accomplishments, milestones, lessons from wins. Not bragging — teaching through experience. "3 years ago I did X, and here's what I learned."

### 10. Demonstrating Competence
"Game respects game." Show deep expertise in action — live breakdowns, real-time problem solving, expert analysis. Hamoors can recognize skill.

### 11. Brand Your Intellectual Property
Name your frameworks. "The Hamoor Protocol" hits different than "my approach to premium clients." See the IP evolution: IP → Media → Data → Software → AI. Named IP compounds.

## Desire Marketing vs Pain Marketing

**Pain Point Marketing** (attracts Planktons):
- "Are you struggling to get clients?"
- "Tired of posting with no results?"
- Attracts people looking to be saved

**Desire Marketing** (attracts Hamoors):
- "Ready to scale to 20K per month?"
- "Want to build a brand people can't ignore?"
- Attracts people who want to be heroes of their own journey

Choose desire marketing. It attracts better clients with better mindsets who stay longer and refer more.
