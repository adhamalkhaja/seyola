# Offer Architecture Rules — From 16 Iterations Generating $1M+

Source: Seyola Research. 16 offer iterations, Aug 2024 – Feb 2026. 289 Stripe transactions, 41 unique customers, $168K+ tracked in dataset period.

## Pricing Findings

- $2,400–$3,600 is a TRANSACTION PSYCHOLOGY NUMBER — the commitment size the buyer is comfortable making in a single decision. It is about the number on the Stripe checkout page, not the total contract value.
- Duration and billing frequency are the levers: $12,000/year → $3,000/quarter. $7,000 total → $3,350/month. Each transaction must land in the $2,400–$3,600 zone.
- $3,200 PIF (Pay In Full) — 24 clients chose this across multiple cohorts
- Most popular payment plan: $900/month × 4 = $3,600 total (66+ clients)
- Lower barrier for volume: $500/month or $1,500 PIF (community tier filled 24 of 36 spots). Below $2,000 = impulse-buy territory.
- Transaction ceiling: $3,600 per checkout — above this, zero clients converted during this period.
- $9,000 PIF and $700/week: zero takers. But the $7,000 client paid $3,350/month — higher totals work when each transaction stays in the zone.
- Always offer both PIF and monthly options. PIF should feel like a discount (4 × $900 = $3,600 vs $3,200 PIF).

## 8-Section Offer Structure (fill-in-the-blank)

1. HOOK: "I'm helping [4–6] more people [scale to / add / build] [$specific target]/month [in timeframe]. Only [4–6] spots."
2. WHO FOR: "This is for [profession] who [situation]. NOT for [disqualifier]." — 3–5 bullets, include at least 1 disqualifier.
3. FRAMEWORK: Name the system. One sentence. Visual if possible. Don't over-explain.
4. MILESTONES: 6 concrete steps with $ anchors. "$10k first client → $20k recurring → $50k pipeline." Each = a mini-promise.
5. SOCIAL PROOF: "[Client name] went from [before] to [$result] in [timeframe]." — minimum 2 proof elements with names + numbers.
6. PRICING: "Option 1: $3,200 Pay In Full (save $400). Option 2: $900/month × 4." Both link to Stripe. No calls, no applications.
7. SCARCITY: "[4–6] spots available. The Stripe link closes when filled." Must be real. Never use "limited time" — use capacity limits.
8. CTA: "Send me 'READY' on WhatsApp and I'll send you the link." Personal, low-friction, conversational. Never "book a call."

## Power Words (appear in every converting offer)
scale, freedom, lifestyle, your terms, imagine, wake up, escape, private, invitation, spots, milestone, weekly calls, 1:1, personal, transform, build, income, revenue, Stripe, results, clients

## Poison Words (killed Era 4)
AI-powered, infrastructure, growth engine, constraint sprint, SRR/PDR/CDR/ORR, leverage, systems architecture, AI-contributed, autopilot, automation framework, "limited time discount"

NOTE: "AI" works as a descriptor ("AI-assisted consulting") or deliverable ("intelligent employee setup"). "AI" as the promise ("AI-contributed profits") kills mentorship conversion.

## Good vs Bad Examples

HOOK — Good: "I'm helping 5 more people scale to $20,000/month" (14 words, specific, personal)
HOOK — Bad: "Unlock $20k–$100k in AI-contributed profits" (abstract, no "I", jargon)
PRICING — Good: "$3,200 PIF or $900/mo × 4. Save $400." (clear, two options, savings shown)
PRICING — Bad: "$5,400 → $3,240 (40% OFF). Limited time." (discount signals desperation)
SCARCITY — Good: "5 spots. Link closes when filled." (real capacity)
SCARCITY — Bad: "Only 5 founders. Offer expires Dec 31." (artificial deadline)

## Duration

- 12–24 week range with the transaction held constant in the $2,400–$3,600 zone.
- 16 weeks at $3,200 PIF was the standard.
- 24 weeks (16+8 bonus) at same price → best month ever ($76k, 30 clients). More value, same transaction size.
- Bonus weeks > discounts. Never discount — extend.

## Anti-Patterns (6 failure modes — each from a real zero-client offer)

1. AI as the headline — Zero mentorship clients when "AI-contributed profits" was core promise.
2. Transaction above $3,600 — $9,000 PIF and $700/week got zero takers.
3. Discounts instead of bonuses — "40% OFF" signaled desperation.
4. Missing "Who For" section — Without it, reader cannot self-select.
5. Artificial deadlines — Calendar tricks vs real capacity limits.
6. Messaging whiplash — Content primes offers with a 3–5 month lag.

KEY: Every anti-pattern appeared in December 2025 (worst month). May 2025 (best month) violated none.

## Payment Intelligence

- Stripe Link fails 34% vs Direct Card 20%. Steer to direct card entry.
- Tuesday is the best day for first payments (22%). Days 13–17 of the month are strongest (37%).
- PIF clients never return. Monthly clients generate 3.3× more lifetime revenue.
- Top 20% of clients = 50% of revenue.

## Retention

- Bimodal: leave in weeks 1–8 OR stay 13–76 weeks. No middle ground.
- Invest everything in first 8 weeks (survival zone).
