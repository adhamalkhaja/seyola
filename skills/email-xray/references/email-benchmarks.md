# Email Broadcast Benchmarks — 153 Broadcasts, $1M+ Revenue

Source: Seyola Research. 153 broadcasts, Sep 2024–Feb 2026. 10,400 subscribers, 4,320 replies, 250+ paying clients.

## The Funnel
10,400 subscribers → 1,882 replied (18%) → 845 replied to offer email (8.1%) → 250 paid (2.4%). The 8,518 who never replied generated zero revenue.

## Email Anatomy — Body Length
U-curve. Short (0-200 words, n=11): 37.7% opens, 2.0 replies. Mid (401-600, n=35): 2.7 replies. Long (1000+, n=28): 10.4 replies — 5× average.

## Email Anatomy — Links
Zero links = 6.3 replies/email. One link = 1.8 replies, 2.64% click (best). 2+ links = 1.5 replies, 1.46% click. Every link is an exit.

## Email Anatomy — PS Line
WITHOUT PS: 4.7 replies, 1.22% click. WITH PS: 2.5 replies, 0.74% click. PS dilutes the single CTA.

## Email Anatomy — Images
0 images = 34.6% open (best). 3+ images = 5.9 replies. Images lower opens but increase replies from openers.

## Content Themes (15 themes, n=153)
- Client results (n=21): 12.6 replies but only 15 conversions
- Consulting angle (n=89): 5.1 replies, 88 conversions — best
- AI angle (n=89): 3.0 replies, 28.8% opens (lowest), 74 conversions
- Storytelling (n=44): 32.5% opens (highest) but 2.2 replies
- Scarcity (n=70): 6.3 replies, 66 conversions
- Quit job (n=57): 5.9 replies, 66 conversions

## Subject Lines
[Brackets] = 36.5% open (best). Statement = 32%. Question = 29.4%. "Doors open" = 25.6% (worst). Sweet spot: 41–60 chars.

## Reply Intelligence
4,320 replies: 50% questions, 28% keyword replies (buying signal), 14% conversation, 5% objections, 3% interest.

## CTA Keywords
"12" (n=33): 59 conversions. ALIEN (n=4): 40.3% open, 15 conversions. WORLD (n=10): 22 conversions.

## Quarterly Decline
Open rate: 41.2% (Q3 2024, 2,770 subs) → 25.1% (Q1 2026, 10,400 subs). List hygiene problem, not deliverability.

## Send Day
Sunday = 33.3% opens (best). Tuesday = 9.3 replies (best for replies). Friday = worst across all metrics.

## Conversion Speed
Median signup-to-payment: 31.5 days. Reply-to-payment: 3 days. Fast converters (≤30d) pay $3,460 avg. Slow (>30d) pay $2,280. Fast buyers pay 52% more.

## Conversion Email DNA
All 13 converting emails are offer emails. Theme over-indexing: quit job +18pp, mentions money +18pp, pain/agitation +16pp. Converting emails: median 860 words, 69% brackets in subject, 62% PS line. Zero conversion emails sent Thursday. 9/13 preceded by warmup email 1-2 days prior.

## Anti-Patterns
1. Links kill replies — 0 links = 6.3 replies vs 1.8 with links
2. "Doors open" subject fatigue — 25.6% open rate
3. AI-topic open penalty — 28.8% vs 32.5% for storytelling
4. Question mark subjects — -1.7 pts vs statements
5. Same-day ghost — sending doc and waiting = death
6. Slow follow-up — fast converters pay 52% more
