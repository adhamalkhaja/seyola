# The Five Ps

## One Person, One Pain, One Promise, One Path, One Platform

Every offer starts with five decisions. Not five categories. Five commitments.

### ONE PERSON

Your [[pfc-avatar-layers|Perfect Future Client]]. Specific, not broad. Not "business owners" — that's everyone. "Solo fitness coaches earning $3-8K/month who want to scale without hiring" — that's someone you can actually help.

You get to CHOOSE who you serve. This is a privilege most people waste by trying to serve everyone.

### ONE PAIN

The 3 major headaches your person lies awake thinking about at 2am. Not surface-level "I need more clients" — the emotional truth underneath: "I feel invisible and I don't know if this is ever going to work."

### ONE PROMISE

The Dream Island outcome. Paint it in 4K HD. What does life look like when the pain is gone?

Key: promise for your AVERAGE client, not your best case study. Small promises are easier to keep AND easier to believe.

### ONE PATH

Your method + milestones. The bridge from Pain to Promise. This becomes your Painkiller Program and signature system.

### ONE PLATFORM

One short-form + one long-form. That's it. Not five platforms.

**Short-form options:**
- Instagram — best if under 50, comfortable on video
- Facebook — best if audience is over 50, or you prefer writing
- LinkedIn — best if serving professionals/B2B

**Long-form options:**
- YouTube — best if solo educator, visual content, or tutorial-heavy
- Podcast — best if interviewer type, or audience consumes on mobile/commute

**Platform philosophy:** Market is NOT saturated. There are enough people on any single platform to build a million-dollar business. Pick ONE short + ONE long. Commit 12+ months.

## The "I Help" Statement

Formula: "I help [person] get [promise] without [pain] through [path]"

Example: "I help solo fitness coaches get their first 10 paying clients in 90 days without cold DMs through the Content Engine system."

This statement goes everywhere: bio, about page, intro on calls, email signature.

## Update Cadence

Review Five Ps 2-4 times per year, especially before promos and launches. Your person may evolve. Your promise may sharpen. The path definitely improves.
