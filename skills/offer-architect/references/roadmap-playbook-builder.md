# Roadmap Builder & 5-Page Playbook

## The Roadmap Builder

Your roadmap comes directly from your [[pain-drivers-painkiller|Painkiller Program]]. Each desired outcome becomes a phase.

### Per Phase, Define:

**Skills needed.** Core 2-3 skills your client needs to learn (not 20).

**Trainings to deliver.** Playbooks, videos, workshops.

**Books to recommend.** 1-2 per phase. More than that and nobody reads any of them.

**Checkpoints to hit.** How does the client know they've completed this phase? Without checkpoints, clients drift. Give them something concrete: "When you've landed your first 3 paying clients, you move to Phase 2."

### The Evolution

- v1 is rough and detailed. Too many steps. That's fine. Get it out of your head.
- v2 you simplify. Remove what doesn't matter. Combine steps.
- v3 and beyond: "dumbass simple" is the goal.

Your roadmap will change every 6 months. That's the process.

> Start rough. Simplify over time. Spend an hour and ship v1.

## The 5-Page Playbook Structure

All playbooks follow the same 5-page format:

**Page 1: OBJECTIVE.** What are we trying to achieve? One sentence. Crystal clear.

**Page 2: PROBLEM.** What is the common mistake? What's going wrong for most people?

**Page 3: CONTEXT.** Why does this problem exist? Build understanding before giving the solution.

**Page 4: SOLUTION.** The framework, formula, or process to overcome it. The core education.

**Page 5: CHECKLIST.** Next steps to implement. Specific action items.

### Deployment

Playbooks double as:
- [[format-examples|Lead magnets]]
- Workshop resources
- [[experience-onboarding|Client deliverables]]

Build once, deploy everywhere. Use Gamma, Notion, Google Docs, or Canva. See the [[offer-blueprint-components|offer blueprint]] for how playbooks fit into the full offer.

> 5 pages. 45 minutes. Published. That's the energy.
