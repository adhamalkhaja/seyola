# Proof Collection & Signature IP

## Two Types of Proof

**Case Study.** Detailed interview or breakdown of a client result. 15-30 minutes. Walk through the full [[golden-client-journey|client journey]]: before, during, after. Tool: Riverside FM.

**Testimonial.** Brief client statement. 30 seconds to 1:30 minutes. Quick and emotional. Tool: Senja.io.

## The Four Questions Framework

1. **What was your situation BEFORE?** Stats, feelings, obstacles. Paint hell.
2. **How were you FEELING?** Emotional state. Frustrations. Fears. Get vulnerable.
3. **What made you decide to COMMIT?** The pivot moment. Why NOW? Why ME?
4. **How does life feel NOW?** Results. Wins. Milestones. Paint heaven.

### The Killer Question
"What was the deciding factor that made you decide to work with me?"

This answer gets future prospects off the fence. Your client articulates it better than you ever could.

### Timing
Strike while the iron is HOT. Collect at the FIRST big win, not 6 months later when the emotion has faded.

### The "Be Proud" Folder
Create a folder on your phone. Screenshot every win immediately. Client messages, revenue screenshots, transformation photos. Deploy for launches, social posts, landing pages, [[offer-blueprint-components|offer docs]].

## Building Your Signature IP

### The Three Pillars of Market Leadership

**PILLAR 1: CLAIM A CATEGORY.**
Ultra-specific: who you serve + what transformation. Players compete on price. Leaders set the price. Narrower claim = louder authority.

**PILLAR 2: NAME & FRAME YOUR METHOD.**
Proprietary name — your [[unique-mechanism]]. Use: System, Engine, Code, Blueprint, Protocol, Framework, Formula. "The Revenue Flywheel" = real. "Ultimate Cash Machine 3000" = late-night infomercial.

**PILLAR 3: PROVE IT & SHOW IT EVERYWHERE.**
Framework without proof is just an opinion. Goal: 5 case study interviews in Year 1. Beta test first, THEN collect proof.

### [[ip-deployment|IP]] Evolution
IP will change 3-4 times in first two years. Don't consume competitor content while building — build from gut and client insights. That's where originality comes from.
