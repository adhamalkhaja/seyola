# The One Simple Offer Blueprint & 5 Components

## The 8-Step Blueprint

A proper offer takes 1-2 weeks of focused work. Not because it's complicated. Because it's IMPORTANT.

1. **Market Research** (~2 hours) — done via offer-strategist
2. **[[five-ps-methodology|The Five Ps]]** (~30 min) — done via offer-profiler
3. **Perfect Future Client** (~1 hour) — done via offer-profiler
4. **[[pain-drivers-painkiller|Painkiller Program]]** (~1 hour) — done via offer-strategist
5. **Offer Blueprint Builder** (~1 hour) — this skill
6. **Roadmap Builder** (~1 hour) — this skill
7. **Offer Document** (~1 DAY, 6-7 hours) — this skill (the big one)
8. **[[launch-sequence-content|Launch & Validation]]** (ongoing) — done via offer-launcher

Sabri Subi's "tip of the spear ([[angle-frameworks|angles]])" concept: over-index your time on your VSL, your offer doc, your mini-series, and your case studies. These are the sales assets that move the needle most. 80% time on sales assets, 20% on product. Flip the common ratio.

## The 5 Offer Components

### 1. PRODUCT
Give it a clear name tied to the outcome. Use words like: System, Engine, Code, Blueprint, Protocol, Framework. Avoid: "course" or "program." ("Freedom Engine" beats "12-Week Program.")

### 2. PROMISE
Tangible + objective North Star. Small promises are easier to keep AND easier to believe. Promise for your AVERAGE client, not best case. "Get your first 5 paying clients in 90 days" beats "make 30K/month."

### 3. URGENCY & SCARCITY
People need a reason to act NOW. Cap spots. Time-bound offers. BTC: 48-72 hours. Core offer: 3-5 days doors open. Rise price quarterly.

### 4. BONUSES
Must be congruent with the core promise. Best bonuses = your TIME. Strategy call in first week. An audit. A done-for-you service. Random unrelated bonuses cheapen the offer.

### 5. GUARANTEE
Action-based guarantees work best: "Get X result or I work with you for free until you do." NOT conditional hoop-jumping with 47 requirements. Eventually, move to "no guarantees" — just case studies.

## The Offer Laws

1. Sell the vacation, not the plane flight.
2. Small promises are easier to keep.
3. Give people a reason to act NOW.
4. Phase the path for easy consumption.

## The 15-Section Offer Document Structure

The offer doc is the single most important sales asset. Spend a full day (6-7 hours) building it.

1. **Headline** — main promise, the outcome
2. **Context + Scarcity** — quick context, urgency, why now
3. **Identity Fork** — "Do you want to be X or Y?" Force a choice
4. **Why This Matters** — founder narrative, why you built this
5. **The Goal** — "Help you X without Y"
6. **The Problem** — call out the market issue, what's broken
7. **The Opportunity** — your new model, your paradigm, what's different
8. **Who This ISN'T For** — qualify OUT the wrong people (makes right people trust more)
9. **Who This IS For** — attract the right people
10. **The Plan** — 3 phases, assets, frameworks, "fast cash sprint" if relevant
11. **Delivery** — how the program operates (calls, community, resources)
12. **Investment** — [[pricing-payment-strategy|price, terms, ROI framing]]
13. **FAQs** — 5-7 common questions answered
14. **Proof** — screenshots, testimonials, case studies, wall of proof
15. **CTA** — next steps, deadline, how to join
