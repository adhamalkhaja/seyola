# ESP Selection & Deliverability

## Choosing Your ESP

Two options. Pick one today. The wrong choice is overthinking this for a month.

**Kit (formerly ConvertKit):** Built for creators. Clean interface. Visual [[automation-segmentation|automation]] builder (drag-and-drop, no code). Excellent deliverability. Cyprus landing page templates. The recommendation for most people starting out.

**Beehive:** Newsletter-first platform. Great referral system for list growth. Clean analytics dashboard. Better if your primary format is a regular newsletter edition.

**NOT recommended:**
- **Klaviyo** — E-commerce focus. Not built for service businesses or creators.
- **MailChimp** — Outdated 2014 UX. Once the default, now the "I picked the first thing I found" choice.
- **GoHighLevel** — Overkill for email. Steep learning curve. Deliverability issues. Only makes sense if you're already deep in their ecosystem.

**The principle:** Engagement matters more than list size. 5K engaged subscribers beats 50K dead ones. Every time.

## DNS Setup Process

Don't DIY this. The $100 saves hours of headaches and potential deliverability disasters.

1. **Buy a sending domain** — Separate from your main website. If your site is `yourbrand.com`, send from `mail.yourbrand.com` or `hello.yourbrand.com`.
2. **Set up Google Workspace** on that sending domain.
3. **Hire a DNS specialist on Fiverr** (~$100). Search "email DNS setup" or "email deliverability."
4. **They configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC** — Don't worry about what these acronyms mean. The specialist handles it.
5. **Done in 1-2 days.** Test by sending to yourself and checking inbox placement.

## Sender Reputation

Your sender reputation is your email credit score. Hard to build, easy to destroy.

Key [[email-kpis|metrics]] that signal health:
- Deliverability ~98% (below 95% = DNS/reputation problem)
- Open rate 40-55% (check after 48 hours, not same day)
- Click rate 2-5%
- Bounce rate below 1%
- Spam complaints below 0.1%

What destroys reputation: sending to dead lists, high bounce rates, spam complaints, sudden volume spikes from new domains. What builds it: consistent sending, high engagement, clean list, proper DNS.

## The Password Reply Hack

The single most powerful deliverability trick. One email. One reply. Trusted sender forever.

**First email after opt-in:** "Reply with the word MANGO to unlock full access"

A single reply marks you as a trusted sender in their email provider. This means inbox placement — not promotions tab, not spam — for ALL future emails from you.

**Pick a fun password word:** Mango, Pineapple, Unicorn, Avocado. NOT "password" or "yes." Fun words get more replies because people smile.

**The opening sequence:**
1. **Password email** (immediate) — Reply request + hint at what's coming
2. **Intro email** (1 hour later) — Set expectations, cadence, what they'll get
3. **Mini-course Day 1** (next morning) — Now they're in primary inbox, warmed up, ready

By the time your [[magnet-stack|mini-course]] starts, you're already a trusted sender. Everything that follows lands in the inbox.
