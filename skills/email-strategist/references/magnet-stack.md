# The Magnet Stack

## High-Engagement vs Low-Engagement

Quality over quantity. High-engagement magnets beat low-engagement ones every time.

**High-engagement:** Email sequences (mini-courses), interactive quizzes, challenges. These create multiple touchpoints and build a relationship over days.

**Low-engagement:** PDFs, checklists, one-page downloads. People download, maybe skim, forget about you. The lead magnet equivalent of a business card.

Start with high-engagement. The mini-course is your foundation.

## The 4 Magnets

Build them in order. Each one adds a new entry point to your list.

### 1. Mini-Course Magnet (Foundation)

An 8-day email sequence. The highest-trust magnet because it delivers value daily and creates a habit of opening your emails.

**Structure:**
- **Day 0:** Password email (the [[esp-deliverability|deliverability hack]])
- **Day 0 + 1 hour:** Intro email (set expectations, cadence, how to ask questions)
- **Days 1-7:** Daily educational emails with case study snippets, personal stories, deep YouTube video links
- **Day 8:** Summary email with key takeaways, Super Signature CTAs, next steps
- **Day 9+:** Nurture emails every 2 days with previously-written content (40+ days of content from your backlog)

**Topic ideas for 7 days** (adapt to your [[five-ps-methodology|niche]]): Leads → Offers → Transformations → Systems → Content → Scale → Proof.

Build it once. Run it forever.

### 2. Free Community Magnet

Toolkits, templates, resource hubs. Quick value, lower commitment than a mini-course.

Best for: people who aren't ready for an 8-day commitment but want something useful now.

### 3. Quiz Magnet

Interactive "What type of X are you?" experience. Data goldmine — responses tell you exactly what segment they belong to.

Typical conversion: 15-25%. People love learning about themselves. Personalized results feel valuable. Enables segmentation for follow-up.

### 4. LinkedIn Comment Magnet

"Comment GUIDE and I'll DM you the link" → DM with landing page → opt-in → Kit tags high-intent ("77").

This is your highest-intent magnet. Someone who comments publicly and follows through to opt-in is a warm lead. The [[automation-segmentation|tag "77"]] signals this to your automation system.

## The Inception Flywheel

Video → Email → YouTube → More subscribers → More video views. Circular. Self-reinforcing.

**The pipeline:**
1. Get YouTube video link (any video from your channel)
2. Extract transcript via noteGPT.io
3. Paste into Script Email Post Wizard GPT (custom GPT)
4. Get subject line options, preview text, email body draft
5. **Refine for human feel** (15 minutes — strip AI patterns, inject personality, add anecdote/client story, make it sound like YOU)
6. Add YouTube deep link via LinkTwin at bottom (opens YouTube app directly, not browser)

**Output:** 1 video = 1-2 email variants. If the content is long, split into two angles.

**The insight:** AI gets 70%. YOU make it 100%. Anyone can paste a transcript. Almost nobody takes the 15 minutes to make it human. That 15 minutes is the superpower.

Also works backwards: a great email becomes a YouTube script.

## Landing Page Formula

**Outcome + Specificity + Differentiation**

Example: "Six to Seven Figures in Seven Days: A daily 3-minute email with exact steps to scale from 6 to 7 figures at 80%+ profit in 12 months without a sales team, calls, or complex funnels."

**Setup:**
- Use Kit Cyprus template (clean, converts well)
- **Email-only form** — No first name field. They put joke names anyway. Every extra field reduces conversions 2-3x.
- Contrasting CTA button color (if page is dark, button is bright)
- One headline. One email field. One button. Less friction = more sign-ups.
